About

Built on the track.
Proven in the field.

Tom Wilson is a Melbourne-based speed and acceleration coach, Commonwealth Youth Games silver medallist (long jump, Apia 2015), and multiple junior Australian and Victorian gold medallist in sprints, long jump, and relays. His PBs include 10.93s (100m), 21.07s (200m), 47.78s (400m), and 7.31m (long jump).

A Level 2 Athletics Australia Coach and Level 1 ASCA-accredited Strength & Conditioning Coach, Tom works with athletes across AFL, soccer, basketball, and track & field — developing explosive acceleration, sprint mechanics, and change-of-direction ability. He was named 2015 Victorian Male Athlete of the Year (U18) for Track and Field.

As Leader of Athletics at St Monica's College Epping — where he led the program to a historic 9/9 aggregate age group shields at SACCSS Athletics — Tom also serves as Head Sprints & Relays Coach for Team Victoria (10-12 age group), HP Manager for Hurstbridge FNC and Yarrambat JFC, and S&C Coach at Calder United SC.

Services

Speed & Agility Clinics

Group sessions for young athletes ages 10-18 at Preston Athletics Club, Reservoir VIC

01

Sprint Mechanics

Technical breakdown of acceleration positions, drive phase mechanics, and top-end speed maintenance tailored to each athlete's sport.

02

Change of Direction

Agility and COD training focused on deceleration control, body positioning, and explosive re-acceleration for field and court sports.

03

Speed Endurance

Sport-specific conditioning that builds the capacity to maintain speed across quarters, halves, and full games when fatigue sets in.

04

Private Coaching

One-on-one sessions with individualised programming, video analysis, and ongoing performance tracking for serious athletes.
